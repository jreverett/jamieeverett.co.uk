WELCOME TO YOUR SHIFT

Keep the grid in balance.

Your job is to match the plant’s output to the green demand line. There is no equipment wear, and no time limit.

  1. Make heat. Adjust reactor power. Heat and steam take time to build or fall.
  2. Make electricity. Open the turbine valve to turn steam into power. Beyond 72%, the available steam limits output.
  3. Keep it cool. More cooling improves heat removal and makes larger plumes when heat is available. Pumps use some electricity.
  4. Read ahead. Weather, time, weekends and news change demand. Prepare before an event starts.

Low cooling can trigger an automatic shutdown. Keep cooling on, then restart below 305°C. Disconnect the grid to see the site switch to emergency lights.

Pause whenever you want. One day takes 15 minutes at 1×. Your shift saves on this device. Difficulty changes the target tolerance and event size.

About the simulation

This is a game with simplified thermal response and separate heat, steam and cooling stages. It is not an operating model of a real plant. The basic power cycle follows the NRC’s explanation of pressurised water reactors. Weather and news are fictional. The site lighting is deliberately dramatised.
